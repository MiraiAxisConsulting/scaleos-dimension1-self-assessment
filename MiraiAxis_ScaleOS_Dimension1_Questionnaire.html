Dimension 1: Leadership, Governance & Strategy Self-Assessment

A structured evaluation of whether your organization has the leadership, governance, and strategic capabilities required to provide direction, make effective decisions, and achieve sustainable growth.

Every growing company eventually discovers that ambition needs a foundation, and that foundation is leadership. This assessment looks honestly at whether your organization has a clear purpose, a real strategy, disciplined governance, and the leadership depth to make good decisions consistently, or whether growth so far has depended on a few individuals holding it all together.

What gets measured gets managed. The value of this assessment comes entirely from the honesty of your answers. A score that looks good but isn't true helps no one, least of all your own leadership team trying to make real decisions about where to invest next.

Please answer every question as accurately as you can, and use the evidence field under each question to note what actually backs up your score, a document, a system, a person who can confirm it. This assessment takes about 30 to 40 minutes to complete.

What the Score Means

1
Initial
No formal process exists. Things happen informally or inconsistently, with no clear ownership.
2
Developing
Some elements exist but are inconsistent, reactive, or dependent on individual effort.
3
Established
A defined process exists and is generally followed, though not tightly systematic.
4
Managed
The process is consistent, documented, tracked, and actively used to guide decisions.
5
Leading
The capability is a genuine organizational strength, continuously refined and embedded in the culture.
